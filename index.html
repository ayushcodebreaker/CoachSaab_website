Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any special equipment or sensors?
No! All you need is your smartphone camera. Coach Saab analyzes your movement directly through the video feed—no wearable sensors, lidar, or special lighting required.
Is the app free to download?
Yes, Coach Saab is free to download and includes basic skeletal tracking. We offer a premium tier that unlocks advanced biomechanical analytics, historical charting, and custom routines.
Does it work in a crowded or messy gym?
Yes. Our AI engine isolates your specific silhouette and focuses entirely on your skeletal structure, actively ignoring background movement, other people, or gym equipment.
Can I track my progress and mobility over time?
Absolutely. Coach Saab logs your joint angles, depth metrics, and overall form score for every single set, allowing you to visualize your technique and mobility improvements on interactive charts.
Do I have to record from a specific angle?
While a direct side-profile is optimal for depth-heavy movements like squats and deadlifts, the engine dynamically adapts to multi-angle recordings (front, 45-degree) for comprehensive analysis.
